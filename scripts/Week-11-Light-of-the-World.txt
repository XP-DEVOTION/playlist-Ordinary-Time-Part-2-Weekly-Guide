Week 11 — The Light of the World

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast where we’re digging into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so thrilled for Week Eleven as we dive into the Light of the World. So grab a cozy spot, maybe a warm drink, and let’s talk about shining for Jesus.

Our scripture today comes from Matthew 5:14:
“You are the light of the world. A town built on a hill cannot be hidden.”

Isn’t that amazing? Jesus calls us the light of the world — reflecting His love and truth to everyone around us. It’s like we’re lanterns, glowing with His light for all to see.

As we chat today, think about this:
How can I shine Jesus’ light this week?

I’ll be honest — sometimes I dim my light without even realizing it. Last week, a friend was going through a tough time, and I almost stayed quiet because I wasn’t sure what to say. But I shared a simple encouraging word, and her face lit up. That moment reminded me that even a small act can shine God’s love brightly.

Have you ever seen your light make a difference for someone?

This week’s reflection challenges us to be Jesus’ light in the world — letting His love shine through our words, our actions, and our kindness, no matter where we are.

Our activity this week:  
Share an encouraging word or do something that reflects Christ’s light to someone.
Maybe send a note to lift someone’s spirits, help out a neighbor, or offer a listening ear.
These small acts can spread Jesus’ love and brighten someone’s day in a powerful way.

Let’s pray:  
Jesus, let me shine Your light, showing Your love to everyone I meet. Amen.

That’s it for Week Eleven, friends. I’d love to hear how you’re shining for Jesus. Maybe share your idea with a friend for inspiration.

Next week, we’re exploring perseverance, and it’s going to encourage us to keep going strong in faith. You don’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
