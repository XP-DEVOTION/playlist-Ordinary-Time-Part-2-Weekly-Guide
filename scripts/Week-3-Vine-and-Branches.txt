Week 3 — The Vine and the Branches

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast where we’re diving into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m thrilled to be with you for Week Three as we explore the Vine and the Branches. So settle in, maybe grab a cozy blanket, and let’s talk about staying connected to Jesus this week.

Our scripture comes from John 15:5, where Jesus says:
“I am the vine; you are the branches. If you remain in me and I in you, you will bear much fruit.”

I love this imagery. Jesus is the strong, life‑giving vine, and we’re the branches drawing everything we need from Him. But here’s the key — we have to stay connected if we want to thrive.

As we chat today, think about this:
What’s helping you stay close to Jesus right now?

I’ll be honest — sometimes I get so caught up in my to‑do list that I forget to plug into the vine. Last week I was rushing through my day, feeling drained, until I paused for a quick prayer. It was like flipping a switch. Suddenly I felt His peace and strength again. It reminded me that staying connected to Jesus isn’t just a nice idea — it’s what keeps us fruitful.

Can you think of a moment when you felt that connection with Him?

This week’s reflection invites us to remain in Jesus, the true vine. That means carving out moments to be with Him — through prayer, reading Scripture, or simply sitting in His presence.

Our activity this week:  
Spend a little extra time in prayer or Scripture reading to deepen your connection with Christ.
Try adding five minutes to your routine.
Read a psalm.
Talk to Jesus about your day.
These small moments can recharge your soul and help you bear fruit for His glory.

Let’s pray:  
Jesus, keep me rooted in You, the true vine, so my life can overflow with Your fruit. Amen.

That’s it for Week Three, friends. I’d love to hear how you’re staying connected to Jesus. Maybe share your plan with a friend to keep you inspired.

Next week, we’re diving into the Greatest Commandment, and it’s going to challenge us to love God with everything we’ve got. You won’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
