Week 4 — The Greatest Commandment

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast, where we’re exploring God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so excited for Week Four as we dive into the Greatest Commandment. So get comfy, maybe grab a journal, and let’s talk about what it really means to love God with everything we’ve got.

Our scripture today comes from Matthew 22:37–38, where Jesus says:
“Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind. This is the first and greatest commandment.”

That’s the heart of it all, isn’t it? Loving God completely — heart, soul, mind. It’s like Jesus is saying, “Give Me your all, and everything else will flow from there.”

As we dive in, ask yourself:
What’s one way I can show my love for God this week?

I’ll be real — sometimes I let distractions pull me away from this kind of love. Last week, I got so caught up in my phone that I skipped my morning prayer time. By midday, I felt off, like I’d missed something vital. But when I finally paused to sing a worship song, it was like my heart realigned with God. That moment reminded me how much He deserves my full devotion.

Can you think of a time when loving God felt like that for you?

This week’s reflection challenges us to prioritize our love for God. It’s about putting Him first — not just in the big moments, but in the everyday choices, the way we think, feel, and respond.

Our activity this week:  
Set aside intentional time each day to worship God through prayer, song, or gratitude.
Try starting your morning with a simple “Thank You, God” list, or sing a favorite hymn.
These small moments can deepen your love for Him and center your day around His presence.

Let’s pray:  
God, help me love You with all my heart, soul, and mind, and let that love shine through everything I do. Amen.

That’s it for Week Four, friends. I’d love to hear how you’re worshiping God this week. Maybe share your favorite way with a friend for a little inspiration.

Next week, we’re exploring the Parable of the Talents, and it’s going to push us to use our gifts for God’s glory. You don’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
