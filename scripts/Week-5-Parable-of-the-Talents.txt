Week 5 — The Parable of the Talents

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast where we’re diving into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m excited for Week 5 as we explore the Parable of the Talents. So grab a cozy seat—maybe even a pen for ideas—and let’s talk about using our gifts for God’s glory.

Our scripture today comes from Matthew 25:21, where the master says:

“Well done, good and faithful servant. You have been faithful with a few things; I will put you in charge of many things.”

This comes from the parable where a master entrusts his servants with gifts and praises those who use them well. It’s a reminder that God has given each of us unique talents—skills, time, passions—to invest in His kingdom.

As we talk today, think about this:
What’s one gift God has given me that I can use this week?

I’ll be honest—sometimes I underestimate what I have to offer. A while back, I thought, “I’m not great at teaching, so I can’t help at church.” But then I volunteered to organize a small event, and it turns out my knack for planning blessed others. It felt like God whispering, “Use what I gave you, and watch what I do with it.”  
Maybe you’ve overlooked a talent too.

This week’s reflection invites us to be faithful stewards of God’s gifts. It’s not about being the best—it’s about being intentional with what we’ve been given.

Our activity this week:  
Identify one talent or skill you have and use it to bless someone.
If you’re a good listener, call a friend who needs to talk.
If you love cooking, share a meal with a neighbor.
Even small acts can make a big impact in God’s kingdom.

Let’s pray:  
God, help me use the gifts You’ve given me to bless others and bring You glory. Amen.

That’s it for Week 5, friends. I’d love to hear how you’re using your talents—maybe tell a friend your plan to help keep it real. Next week, we’re diving into The Bread of Life, and it’s going to fill your soul with Jesus’ presence. You won’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
