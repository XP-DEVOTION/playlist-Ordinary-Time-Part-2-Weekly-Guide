Week 15 — The Cost of Discipleship

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast where we’re diving into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so pumped for Week Fifteen as we explore the cost of discipleship. So grab a cozy spot, maybe a journal, and let’s talk about what it means to follow Jesus fully.

Our scripture today comes from Luke 14:27:
“And whoever does not carry their cross and follow me cannot be my disciple.”

Jesus doesn’t sugarcoat it. Following Him means sacrifice — taking up our cross and giving Him our all. It’s a call to surrender everything to Him.

As we chat today, think about this:
What’s one thing Jesus might be asking me to let go of this week?

I’ll be honest — surrender is tough for me sometimes. A while back, I was clinging to my need to control my schedule, saying yes to everything except prayer time. Then I felt God nudge me to let go of a few commitments so I could focus on Him. It wasn’t easy, but saying “no” to some things freed me to say “yes” to Jesus in a deeper way.

Have you ever had to let go of something to follow Him more closely?

This week’s reflection challenges us to consider what Jesus is calling us to surrender. It might be a habit, a fear, or even a plan we’re holding too tightly.

Our activity this week:  
Identify one area of your life to surrender to God, and pray about it daily this week.
Maybe it’s worry, a grudge, or a pattern that’s weighing you down.
Name it — and bring it to God each day.
This act of surrender can open your heart to follow Jesus more fully.

Let’s pray:  
Jesus, help me take up my cross and surrender to You, trusting Your path for me. Amen.

That’s it for Week Fifteen, friends. I’d love to hear what you’re surrendering to God — maybe share it with a friend for support.

Next week, we’re diving into the power of prayer, and it’s going to show us how to find peace in God’s presence. You don’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
