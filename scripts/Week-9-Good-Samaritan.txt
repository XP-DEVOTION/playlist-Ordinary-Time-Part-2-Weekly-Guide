Week 9 — The Good Samaritan

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast where we’re diving into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so pumped for Week Nine as we explore the Good Samaritan. So grab a cozy spot, maybe a coffee, and let’s talk about showing compassion to the people around us.

Our scripture today comes from Luke 10:33:
“But a Samaritan, as he traveled, came where the man was; and when he saw him, he took pity on him.”

This moment from Jesus’ parable shows someone who didn’t just pass by — he stopped, cared, and helped someone in need, even when it wasn’t convenient. It’s a challenge to love our neighbors with real, practical action.

As we chat today, think about this:
Who is someone I can show compassion to this week?

I’ll be honest — sometimes I miss these opportunities. Last week, I saw a coworker who looked really down, but I kept walking, thinking, “I’m too busy.” Later, I regretted it and sent her a quick message to check in. That small gesture meant so much to her, and it reminded me how compassion can change someone’s day.

Have you ever had a moment where a simple act of kindness made a difference?

This week’s reflection urges us to be like the Good Samaritan — looking for ways to show love and care. It’s about noticing the people around us and stepping up, even in small ways.

Our activity this week:  
Help someone in need — with a kind word, a donation, or a helping hand.
Maybe offer to listen to a friend, support a coworker, or give a little to a local charity.
These acts of compassion can shine God’s love into someone’s life.

Let’s pray:  
God, make me a Good Samaritan — ready to show Your love and compassion to those who need it. Amen.

That’s it for Week Nine, friends. I’d love to hear how you’re spreading kindness. Maybe share your idea with a friend for inspiration.

Next week, we’re diving into gratitude, and it’s going to transform how we see God’s blessings. You don’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
