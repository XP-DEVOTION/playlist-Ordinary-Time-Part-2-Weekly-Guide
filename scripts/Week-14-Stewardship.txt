Week 14 — Stewardship

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast, where we’re diving into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so excited for Week Fourteen as we talk about stewardship. So grab a cozy spot, maybe a notebook, and let’s explore how we can use God’s gifts wisely.

Our scripture today comes from 1 Peter 4:10:
“Each of you should use whatever gift you have received to serve others, as faithful stewards of God’s grace in its various forms.”

This verse reminds us that everything we have — our time, our talents, our resources — is a gift from God, meant to be used to bless others.

As we chat today, think about this:
What’s one gift I can share to serve someone this week?

I’ll be honest — sometimes I hold back on sharing what I’ve been given. Last month, I hesitated to volunteer for a community project because I felt too busy. But when I finally gave a couple of hours, I saw how it lifted others up — and it brought me joy too. It reminded me that being a good steward isn’t just about giving; it’s about trusting God with what He’s placed in my hands.

Have you ever seen your gifts make a difference when you shared them?

This week’s reflection challenges us to be faithful stewards of God’s gifts. It’s about using what we have — whether it’s a skill, a moment, or a dollar — to serve others and show God’s grace.

Our activity this week:  
Donate time, money, or resources to a cause that serves others.
Maybe give an hour to a local charity, help someone with a skill you have, or support a ministry or neighbor in need.
These acts of stewardship can spread God’s love and make a real impact.

Let’s pray:  
God, help me use Your gifts faithfully to serve others and share Your grace. Amen.

That’s it for Week Fourteen, friends. I’d love to hear how you’re stewarding your gifts — maybe share your idea with a friend for encouragement.

Next week, we’re diving into the cost of discipleship, and it’s going to push us to follow Jesus with all we’ve got. You don’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
