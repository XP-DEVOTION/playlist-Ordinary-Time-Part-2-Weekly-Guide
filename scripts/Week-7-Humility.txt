Week 7 — Humility

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast, where we’re diving into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so thrilled for Week Seven as we talk about humility. So grab a cozy spot, maybe a cup of tea, and let’s explore what it means to put others first.

Our scripture today comes from Philippians 2:3:
“Do nothing out of selfish ambition or vain conceit. Rather, in humility value others above yourselves.”

That’s a high calling, right? Jesus modeled this perfectly — always serving, always lifting others up. Humility isn’t about thinking less of yourself; it’s about thinking of yourself less.

As we chat today, ask yourself:
Where can I show humility in my interactions this week?

I’ll be honest — humility doesn’t always come naturally. Just the other day, I was in a meeting and really wanted to share my ideas first. But I caught myself and decided to listen instead. Letting someone else take the spotlight felt small but powerful, like I was following Jesus’ example in a simple, real way.

Have you had a moment where stepping back made a difference?

This week’s reflection challenges us to practice humility in our daily interactions. It’s about choosing to value others — letting someone else shine, serving quietly, or offering kindness without expecting applause.

Our activity this week:  
Let someone else go first — in line, in a conversation, or even in a decision. Try it once and notice how it shifts your heart. This small act of humility can reflect Jesus’ love in a big way.

Let’s pray:  
Jesus, teach me to walk in humility, putting others first just as You did. Amen.

That’s it for Week Seven, friends. I’d love to hear how you’re practicing humility. Maybe share your story with a friend for a little inspiration.

Next week, we’re diving into the Prodigal Son, and it’s going to remind us of God’s incredible mercy. You don’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
