Week 1 — The Parable of the Sower

Hey friends, welcome to the very first episode of our weekly podcast, where we’re digging into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m Eric, your host, and I’m so excited to start this journey with you. For Week 1, we’re diving into the Parable of the Sower.

So find a cozy spot, maybe grab a coffee, and let’s talk about how God is speaking to us today.

Our scripture comes from Mark 4:14:
“The farmer sows the word.”

I love this image. It’s like God is out there scattering His Word like seeds across a field—full of hope, full of intention, full of possibility. But as Jesus explains in Mark 4:1–20, the real focus of the parable is the soil.

And guess what?
That soil is us.  
Our hearts are the soil where God’s Word lands.

As we talk today, think about this:
What kind of soil is your heart right now?

Jesus describes four types of soil:
– The hard path, where the seed gets snatched away
– The rocky ground, where it sprouts quickly but fades
– The thorny ground, where worries choke it out
– And the good soil, where the Word takes root and produces a beautiful harvest

I’ll be honest—last week, I was thorny soil. I got so caught up in work stress that I barely opened my Bible. But when I finally sat down and read this parable, it felt like God whispering, “Hey, clear out those thorns and let My Word grow again.”  
Maybe you’ve been there too.

This week is all about becoming good soil for God’s Word. It’s about making space for His truth to take root—maybe by setting aside a few quiet minutes to read Scripture, pray, or simply breathe and listen.

Our activity this week:  
Read Mark 4:1–20 and journal about the state of your heart.
Grab a notebook or your phone and write honestly:
– What’s going on inside me?
– Are there distractions I need to clear out?
– Am I seeing any fruit already?

Journaling can help you notice what’s blocking God’s Word and invite Him to soften your heart.

Let’s pray:  
God, soften my heart to receive Your Word and let it grow into something beautiful. Amen.

That’s it for Week 1, friends. I’d love to hear which soil you relate to this week. Maybe jot it down and share it with a friend—it might encourage both of you.

Next week, we’re diving into Faith in Action, and trust me, you won’t want to miss how small steps can make your faith come alive.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
