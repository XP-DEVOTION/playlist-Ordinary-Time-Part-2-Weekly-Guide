Week 8 — The Prodigal Son

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast where we’re exploring God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so excited for Week Eight as we dive into the Parable of the Prodigal Son. So settle in, maybe grab a journal, and let’s talk about God’s boundless mercy.

Our scripture today comes from Luke 15:20:
“But while he was still a long way off, his father saw him and was filled with compassion for him; he ran to his son, threw his arms around him and kissed him.”

This moment is so powerful. The father doesn’t wait. He runs to embrace his wayward son. It’s a picture of God’s love for us — no matter how far we’ve wandered, His mercy moves toward us first.

As we chat today, think about this:
What’s a struggle I can bring back to God this week?

I’ll be real — there are times I feel just like that prodigal son. A while back, I was wrestling with some bad choices and felt too ashamed to pray. But when I finally whispered, “God, I’m sorry,” it was like He ran toward me with open arms. That grace changed everything.

Have you ever felt God’s mercy pull you back when you thought you’d gone too far?

This week’s reflection invites us to return to God with whatever we’re carrying, trusting His mercy. No matter what we’ve done, He’s waiting to welcome us home.

Our activity this week:  
Read Luke 15:11–32, the full parable of the prodigal son, and reflect on God’s love for you.
Grab a notebook and jot down how His mercy feels personal in your life.
This simple reflection can remind you that you are never too far from His embrace.

Let’s pray:  
Father, thank You for Your endless mercy. Welcome me back into Your arms, no matter where I’ve been. Amen.

That’s it for Week Eight, friends. I’d love to hear how God’s love is speaking to you. Maybe share your thoughts with a friend for encouragement.

Next week, we’re exploring the Good Samaritan, and it’s going to inspire us to show compassion in new ways. You won’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
