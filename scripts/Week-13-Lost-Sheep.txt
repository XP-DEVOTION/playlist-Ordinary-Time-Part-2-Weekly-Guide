Week 13 — The Lost Sheep

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast where we’re diving into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so thrilled for Week Thirteen as we explore the Parable of the Lost Sheep. So grab a cozy spot, maybe a warm drink, and let’s talk about Jesus’ relentless love.

Our scripture today comes from Luke 15:4:
“Suppose one of you has a hundred sheep and loses one of them. Doesn’t he leave the ninety‑nine in the open country and go after the lost sheep until he finds it?”

This verse paints such a vivid picture — Jesus, the Good Shepherd, seeking out the one who’s lost with unstoppable love. He doesn’t give up. He doesn’t wait. He goes after the one who wandered.

As we chat today, think about this:
Who in my life might feel lost and need encouragement this week?

I’ll be honest — sometimes I overlook people who are struggling. A while back, I noticed a friend hadn’t been around much, but I assumed they were just busy. Then I reached out with a simple text, and it turned out they were feeling really isolated. That small check‑in opened the door to a meaningful conversation. It showed me how much a little love can mean.

Have you ever reached out to someone and seen God’s love at work?

This week’s reflection calls us to follow Jesus’ example — reaching out to those who feel lost with His love. It’s about noticing the people around us who need a touch of care.

Our activity this week:  
Reach out to someone who might feel lonely or lost and offer encouragement.
Maybe call a friend you haven’t talked to in a while, send a thoughtful message, or invite someone to coffee.
This simple act can show them they’re seen and loved by God through you.

Let’s pray:  
Jesus, thank You for seeking me when I was lost. Help me share Your love with others who need it. Amen.

That’s it for Week Thirteen, friends. I’d love to hear who you’re reaching out to — maybe share your plan with a friend for inspiration.

Next week, we’re diving into stewardship, and it’s going to challenge us to use our gifts wisely for God. You don’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
