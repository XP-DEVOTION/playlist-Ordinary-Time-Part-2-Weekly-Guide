Week 6 — Diving Into the Bread of Life

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast where we’re soaking up God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so glad you’re here for Week 6: “Diving Into the Bread of Life.”

So find a comfy spot, maybe light a candle, and let’s talk about how Jesus sustains us.

Our scripture today comes from John 6:35, where Jesus declares:

“I am the Bread of Life. Whoever comes to me will never go hungry, and whoever believes in me will never be thirsty.”

Isn’t that beautiful? Jesus isn’t just talking about physical hunger. He’s talking about the deep, soul-level nourishment only He can give. He’s inviting us to come to Him and be filled.

As we chat today, ask yourself:
“How am I coming to Jesus for nourishment right now?”

I’ll be real—sometimes I try to fill my soul with other things. Just last week I was stressed and spent way too much time scrolling social media, hoping it would lift my spirits. Spoiler: it didn’t. But when I paused to pray and read a verse, it felt like Jesus handed me a fresh loaf of peace.
That’s what the Bread of Life does.

Can you think of a moment when Jesus filled you up like that?

This week’s reflection is all about coming to Jesus for spiritual nourishment—leaning on Him to satisfy our deepest needs, especially when we feel empty or overwhelmed.

Our activity this week:  
If you can, attend a Eucharist service. If not, spend some quiet time reflecting on Christ’s presence in your life. Set aside a few minutes to sit still and simply say:
“Jesus, You’re my Bread of Life. Fill me.”  
That simple prayer can reconnect you to His sustaining love.

Let’s pray:  
Jesus, You are the Bread of Life. Nourish my soul with Your presence today and always. Amen.

That’s it for Week 6, friends. I’d love to hear how you’re connecting with Jesus this week. Share your moment with a friend—it might encourage them too.

Next week, we’re exploring humility, and it’s going to challenge us in a powerful way. You won’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
