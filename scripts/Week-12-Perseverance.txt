Week 12 — Perseverance

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast where we’re diving into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so excited for Week Twelve as we talk about perseverance. So find a comfy spot, maybe grab a journal, and let’s explore what it means to keep going strong in faith.

Our scripture today comes from Galatians 6:9:
“Let us not become weary in doing good, for at the proper time we will reap a harvest if we do not give up.”

This verse is like a pep talk from God: Keep doing good, even when it’s tough — because there’s a harvest coming. It’s all about staying faithful over the long haul.

As we chat today, think about this:
What’s one good thing I can keep pushing through with this week?

I’ll be real — perseverance can feel hard. Last month, I was trying to stick with daily prayer, but some mornings I just wanted to hit snooze. Then I remembered this verse and kept at it, even when I didn’t feel like it. Slowly, those prayers became a lifeline, strengthening my faith.

Have you ever pushed through something tough and seen God work through it?

This week’s reflection encourages us to keep doing good, trusting God’s timing for the harvest. It’s about not giving up, even when the results aren’t instant.

Our activity this week:  
Choose one good habit — like daily prayer, reading Scripture, or acts of kindness — and commit to it this week, even if it’s challenging.
Pick something small and stick with it.
Maybe pray for five minutes each morning.
Staying consistent can build your faith and show you God’s faithfulness.

Let’s pray:  
God, give me strength to keep doing good, trusting You for the harvest ahead. Amen.

That’s it for Week Twelve, friends. I’d love to hear what habit you’re sticking with — maybe share it with a friend for accountability.

Next week, we’re diving into the Lost Sheep, and it’s going to remind us of Jesus’ relentless love. You don’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
