Week 10 — Gratitude

Hey friends, welcome back to our weekly podcast, where we’re diving into God’s Word together, one theme at a time. I’m your host, and I’m so excited for Week Ten as we talk about gratitude. So settle in, maybe grab a notebook, and let’s explore how thankfulness can transform our hearts.

Our scripture today comes from 1 Thessalonians 5:18:
“Give thanks in all circumstances, for this is God’s will for you in Christ Jesus.”

That’s a bold call, isn’t it? Giving thanks in all circumstances — the good, the tough, the messy. Gratitude shifts our perspective. It helps us see God’s hand even when life feels heavy.

As we chat today, think about this:
What’s one thing I’m thankful for right now?

I’ll be real — gratitude doesn’t always come naturally. Last week, I was frustrated with a delayed project and grumbling to myself. But then I paused and thanked God for my team’s hard work. That tiny shift changed my whole mood. It was like gratitude opened my eyes to blessings I had overlooked.

Have you ever noticed how thankfulness changes your outlook?

This week’s reflection invites us to let gratitude shape our perspective. It’s about choosing to thank God even when it’s tough — and watching how that choice draws us closer to Him.

Our activity this week:  
Make a list of ten things you’re thankful for, and thank God for each one.
Grab a piece of paper or your phone and jot down anything — big wins, small joys, even challenges.
This simple list can fill your heart with joy and remind you of God’s goodness.

Let’s pray:  
God, fill my heart with gratitude for Your blessings in every moment, big or small. Amen.

That’s it for Week Ten, friends. I’d love to hear what you’re thankful for — maybe share your list with a friend for encouragement.

Next week, we’re diving into the Light of the World, and it’s going to inspire you to shine for Jesus. You don’t want to miss it.

Until then, keep growing in God’s Word.
